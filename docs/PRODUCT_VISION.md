# Product Vision

## The purpose

Kannadada Beru is a living cultural museum for Karnataka's words, places, voices, and memories. A visitor can begin with Many Living Voices or a district, discover a regional expression, understand its meaning and context, and hear a credited human voice where suitable audio exists.

## The experience

Regional Kannada is often learned through family, neighbourhood, work, travel, and oral tradition. Kannadada Beru brings that knowledge into a welcoming digital space without separating an expression from its place or source.

## Product principles

1. **Kannada leads the interface.** English and Romanization improve access without replacing Kannada.
2. **Place provides context.** Districts and regions help visitors navigate cultural stories.
3. **Every source stays visible.** Dictionary entries, regional examples, and recordings retain clear attribution.
4. **Human voices keep their identity.** General Kannada audio is labelled without a regional claim. A regional Play control appears only with matching transcript, self-reported speaker origin, credit, reuse rights, speaker approval and second human review. Tulu and other coastal languages remain distinct from Kannada.
5. **Learning should feel joyful.** Search, maps, comparison, and Pada Beru turn exploration into participation.
6. **Privacy remains understandable.** Contribution drafts stay on the visitor's device until deliberately exported.

## Audiences

- Kannada speakers curious about regional language and cultural context
- Students, educators, researchers, and writers
- Families and diaspora communities exploring Kannada through English or Romanization
- Visitors discovering Karnataka through its districts and living languages

## Product identity

The visual system draws from Karnataka's red and gold palette, stone inscriptions, landscape, and the Kannada-root emblem. The result is calm, warm, and unmistakably connected to the land behind the language.
